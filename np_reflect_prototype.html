Before you start

Here's what happens with what you share.

Who can see your reflections
Your CCA advisor and the Sandbox team can see your reflections and ratings, with your name. They use them to learn what helps students grow, and to offer help when it would make a difference. Sandbox also uses what students write, across all schools, to decide which programmes to keep, change or add.
AI follow-up questions
An AI reads each reflection to ask you one follow-up question. You can switch this off.
Only you see your growth web in full detail. You can ask to see or change your data at any time.
